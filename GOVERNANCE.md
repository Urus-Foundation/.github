# Governance

## Overview

The Urus Foundation is an open-source organization that develops and maintains the Urus programming language and its ecosystem. This document describes how the project is governed.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers have full commit access and are responsible for:
- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Triaging issues
- Making release decisions
- Guiding the project's technical direction

### Contributors

Contributors are anyone who submits a pull request, files an issue, or participates in discussions. All contributions are valued and reviewed by maintainers.

### Community Members

Anyone who uses Urus, participates in discussions, or helps others is a valued community member.

## Decision Making

- **Minor changes** (bug fixes, small improvements) are decided by individual maintainers through PR review.
- **Significant changes** (new language features, breaking changes) require discussion in a GitHub issue or discussion thread and approval from at least two maintainers.
- **Major decisions** (roadmap changes, governance changes) are discussed openly and decided by consensus among maintainers.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions and alignment with the project's goals may be invited to become maintainers. This is decided by existing maintainers through consensus.

## Code of Conduct

All participants in the Urus Foundation community are expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Changes to Governance

This governance model may evolve as the project grows. Changes require consensus among maintainers and will be communicated transparently to the community.
